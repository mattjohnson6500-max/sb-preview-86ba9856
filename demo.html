What you've taught it

    How it works

    Your second brain is a folder on your computer that Claude keeps organized, only when you ask. It learns from your corrections, so it gets closer to right over time.

    1. It's yours alone

      It lives on your computer and runs on your own Claude account. Matt set it up but can't see, change or share anything in it. Training on your chats is switched off in your Claude privacy settings.

    2. It only does what you ask

      It never starts work on its own. Before changing anything, it tells you the plan and waits for your OK. It never sends, shares or deletes.

    3. Expect the first try to be wrong

      Everything it makes is a draft from a new assistant. Your corrections are how it learns.

    4. The goal: no changes needed

      A win is when you review a draft and approve it as is. The win count on My Requests shows the trend.

    5. You're the professional

      For codes, fire ratings and life safety it shows what's in your files, with sources. Final judgment is yours.

    6. You own it, no strings

      Nothing to pay anyone beyond your own Claude plan. Want it to work differently or do something new? Just ask, and it changes itself after your OK.

    7. Help is always there

      Ask "How do I…?" for step-by-step instructions. Can't find a button? Drop a screenshot into the chat and it will point to it.
