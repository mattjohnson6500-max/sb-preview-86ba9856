Quick Start

Connect your folder in the Claude app and say "Open my second brain." After that, just talk normally. These are examples, not commands to memorize.

What to say

When you want to… Say something like…
Start "Open my second brain."
See what it can do "What can you do?" or "Give me the tour."
Get a task done Just ask: "Compare this substitution to what we specified." It will tell you what it knows and ask if there's anything else to read first.
Give it files to read Drop them in the Inbox folder and say "I put them in the Inbox."
Approve a draft "Looks good" or "Approved."
Correct a draft Say what's wrong: "Warranty should be its own row." It will ask whether to do it that way every time.
Save a fact "Remember that our hardware rep is now Dana Ruiz."
Find something "What do we know about fire-rated glazing?"
Recall a decision "What did we decide about the roof on Fairview?"
Capture a meeting "Here are my notes from today's OAC meeting…" (paste or talk)
See everything "Show my dashboard," or double-click Dashboard.html in your folder
Tidy up "Do a weekly checkup."
Undo "Undo that."

It always shows you the plan and waits for your OK before filing or changing anything. It never sends, shares or deletes.

When you're stuck, or want something different

Ask for step-by-step help. "How do I connect my folder?" or "How do I open the dashboard?" It gives you one step at a time and waits for you to say "done."

Send a screenshot. Can't find a button? Take a picture of your screen and drop it into the chat. It will tell you exactly where to click.

On a Mac On Windows
Press Shift + Command + 4, drag over the area, then drag the picture into the chat. Press Windows + Shift + S, drag over the area, then click in the chat and press Ctrl + V.

Change how it works. It's yours, with no strings. Ask for anything: "Can you also track warranty terms?" or "Stop asking about sources for emails." It explains the change, waits for your OK, and you can undo it anytime.

Ask about privacy. "Who can see this?" It will explain plainly.

Connect your email or other apps (optional). Say "How do I connect my Gmail?" (or Google Drive, or your calendar) and it walks you through it. Then you can ask things like "Find the rep's email about the hinge lead time" or "Draft a reply to that RFI." It only drafts. You send.
