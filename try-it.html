Try It Yourself

The demo only shows the flow. To actually chat with a second brain, try it in your own Claude account with a fictional sample practice: made-up clients, projects, products and meeting notes. It takes about 5 minutes and installs nothing.

Download the sample pack

Steps

  1. Go to claude.ai and sign in with your own Claude account (Pro or higher).
  2. In the left sidebar, click Projects, then create a new project. Name it "Second Brain Demo."
  3. Unzip the sample pack. Open Project Instructions (paste this).txt, copy all of it, and paste it into the project's instructions box.
  4. Add all the files from the Try It - Sample Files folder to the project's files (drag them in).
  5. Start a chat in the project and say "Hi." Then try the ideas below.

If any button looks different, take a screenshot, drop it into a regular Claude chat, and ask where to click.

Things to try

  • "The contractor sent a substitution request for the air barrier on Riverside Library. Compare it to what we specified."
  • "What did we decide at the last Elm Street Lofts OAC meeting, and what are my action items?"
  • "Check the amenity level hardware sets against our door hardware lessons."
  • "Draft a reply to the contractor on RFI 014."
  • Correct a draft and see what happens: "Put the warranty in its own row."
  • "Who can see this?" or "How would this work with my real projects?"

How this differs from the real thing
