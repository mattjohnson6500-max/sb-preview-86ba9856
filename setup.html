Setup Guide

About 20 minutes. Do these once, in order. If anything on screen looks a little different from what's described here, look for the closest match, since the Claude app gets updated often. From Step 5 on, you can ask Claude for help with anything, including by sending a screenshot.

Download the starter folder

What you need

  • A Mac or Windows computer
  • A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher)
  • The Amy's Second Brain starter folder (the download above)

Steps

  1. Install the Claude desktop app and check your privacy setting. Go to claude.com/download, install the app for your computer, and sign in with your own Claude account. Then open Settings, find Privacy, and turn off the option that lets your chats be used to improve Claude's models. Do this before you put anything in. Also, skip the thumbs up and thumbs down buttons on work chats, since those send the conversation to Anthropic as feedback.
  2. Put the folder in place. Double-click the downloaded zip file to unzip it. Move the Amy's Second Brain folder into your Documents folder.
    • If your Documents folder is backed up by iCloud (Mac) or OneDrive (Windows), that's your backup taken care of.
    • Make sure the files stay on your computer, not "in the cloud only." On a Mac, right-click the folder and choose Keep Downloaded if you see it. On Windows, right-click it and choose Always keep on this device.
  3. Connect the folder to Claude. Open the Claude app and start a new chat. Look for the option to add or choose a folder, usually a folder icon or an Add folder button near the message box. Pick Amy's Second Brain and click Allow when Claude asks for permission. Claude can only see this one folder.

  4. Tell Claude about the folder, once. In Claude's Settings, find the box for your personal preferences. Paste this in and save:

    When my "Amy's Second Brain" folder is connected, always read CLAUDE.md in that folder first and follow it.

  5. Say hello. With your folder connected, type or say "Open my second brain." It explains the ground rules and offers a 2-minute tour. Ask it anything, like "What can you do?" or "Who can see this?" There's no questionnaire. It learns about you as you work.

  6. Try one real task. Pick something you'd do this week, like a substitution request or a hardware schedule check, and ask for it. When it asks if there's anything else to read, drop the relevant files in the Inbox folder. Review the draft, correct it, and approve it. Then double-click Dashboard.html to see it recorded.

Talking instead of typing: click the microphone button in the message box and speak. Replies are kept short so they work well out loud.

Your dashboard

Double-click Dashboard.html in your folder to open it in your web browser. It stays on your computer and updates every time Claude finishes something. It has three views: My Requests (everything you've asked for and what's waiting on you), My Brain (everything it knows, with sources, plus every lesson you've taught it), and How it works. You can also say "Show my dashboard" in chat. The demo shows what it looks like with a few weeks of sample work.

Optional extras

Browsing your notes with Obsidian (free). If you'd ever like to look through your notes yourself, install Obsidian from obsidian.md, choose Open folder as vault, and pick Amy's Second Brain. It's purely for looking. You never have to use it.

From your phone. Filing happens at your desk. On the go, you can drop a file or photo into the Inbox folder through iCloud Drive or OneDrive on your phone, then say "I put something in the Inbox" when you're back.

Connecting Gmail, Google Drive or other apps. Claude can be connected to services like Gmail, Google Drive and your calendar, depending on your Claude plan. Ask your second brain, "How do I connect my Gmail?" and it will walk you through it one step at a time. Once connected, it can search your email for a rep's message or draft a reply, but it never sends anything. You can disconnect anytime.

If something goes wrong

Problem Fix
Claude says it can't see your files Start a new chat and connect the folder again (Step 3).
Claude doesn't seem to follow the rules Say "Read CLAUDE.md first," then ask again.
A file "won't open" Make sure it's fully downloaded to your computer (Step 2), then try again.
An answer looks wrong Say "That's wrong" and give the correct fact. It fixes the note and records the lesson.
You're stuck Ask "How do I…?" for one step at a time. Can't find a button? Take a screenshot (Mac: Shift + Command + 4; Windows: Windows + Shift + S) and drop it into the chat.

You don't need to edit any files yourself. To change anything about how it works, just tell Claude.
